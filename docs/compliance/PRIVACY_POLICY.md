# Pramana Institute of Yogic Studies — Privacy Policy
**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026  
**Governing Regulations:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India) & General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR)

---

## 1. Introduction and Fiduciary Commitment
Pramana Institute of Yogic Studies ("Pramana", "Institute", "we", "us") operates an authentic classical Himalayan Gurukula learning management and certification platform. We respect the privacy of our Sadhakas, Students, Faculty, Acharyas, and visitors. This Privacy Policy details the categories of personal data we collect, lawful purposes for processing, data protection safeguards, and your rights as a Data Principal under the DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect
We collect personal data strictly necessary to facilitate education, residential ashram sadhana, and accredited certification:

| Category | Data Elements Collected | Lawful Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Identity & Contact** | Full Name, Email, Phone/WhatsApp, Mailing Address, Emergency Contact | Contractual Necessity (Enrollment) & Consent |
| **Academic & Learning** | Class attendance, lesson progress, quiz attempts, practical video submissions, assignment grades | Educational Contract & Certification Evaluation |
| **Billing & Invoices** | Billing address, GSTIN (if applicable), payment transaction references, gateway order IDs (*Never raw credit card details*) | Legal Obligation (GST Act / Accounting Rules) |
| **Certification** | Conferred certificate number, public verification token, issue dates, course completion evaluation snapshots | Legitimate Interest & Accreditation Verification |
| **Technical & Telemetry** | IP address, User Agent, device type, cookie preferences, audit logs | Legitimate Interest & Network Security |

---

## 3. Lawful Purpose and Data Minimization
Your personal data is processed solely for:
1. Administering enrolled academic courses, residential retreats, and live satsang sessions.
2. Generating and maintaining tamper-evident, verifiable certificates (Yoga Alliance RYS and YCB Ayush Ministry accredited).
3. Issuing tax invoices and complying with statutory tax audits in India.
4. Sending timely reminders (class schedules, assignment deadlines, fee payments) based on user channel preferences.
5. Detecting and mitigating fraudulent activity, security vulnerabilities, and unauthorized access.

---

## 4. Third-Party Data Processors (Sub-Processors)
We engage vetted third-party data processors bound by Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):
- **Stripe & Razorpay:** PCI-DSS Level 1 payment gateway processing.
- **Bunny.net:** Encrypted, tokenized video streaming delivery with geo-fencing.
- **WhatsApp Business API:** Session rescheduling notifications and reminders.
- **Postmark / Amazon SES:** Transactional notification delivery.

We **never** sell, rent, or monetize student data with third-party advertisers.

---

## 5. Data Principal & Subject Rights
As a Data Principal under DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR, you possess the right to:
1. **Right to Access & Confirmation:** Obtain confirmation whether your personal data is being processed, along with a summary of data categories and sharing entities.
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure:** Request rectification of inaccurate records or erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for educational or statutory audit purposes.
3. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Lodge inquiries or complaints with our designated Data Protection Officer.
4. **Right to Nominate:** Nominate any other individual to exercise your rights in event of death or incapacity.
